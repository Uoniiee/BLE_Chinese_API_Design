## Findings

1. Log export is usable for diagnosis.
   The exported files include enough continuous lines to replace screenshot/OCR
   handoff for this workflow.

2. Local stop behavior is correct.
   Both A and C clear peers and readiness immediately after `stop-click`.

3. The session guard is effective.
   C shows a late scan callback after stop, and the API ignores it instead of
   allowing it to repopulate peer state.

4. Remote disconnect business readiness is correct.
   C sees stale physical `connected=1 writable=1` after A stops, but business
   readiness remains `ready=0`, and sends are skipped.

5. Reconnect and burst sends recover.
   After manual restart, both sides return to `ready=1` and exchange short and
   burst messages successfully.

6. Minor reconnect flicker remains observable but not blocking.
   On C, reconnect briefly moves from `ready=1 (2/1)` to `连接中 (2/1)` and then
   back to `ready=1 (2/2)` while the second physical path finishes setup. No
   failed user send was observed in that window. For business UI, this can be
   smoothed later if it becomes visually distracting.

## Decision

v0.6.7-debug passes the current diagnostic target:

- exported logs are readable and complete enough for handoff;
- local stop does not accidentally restore readiness;
- stale callbacks after stop are ignored;
- remote stop does not drift back to business-ready;
- reconnect and burst sends recover.
