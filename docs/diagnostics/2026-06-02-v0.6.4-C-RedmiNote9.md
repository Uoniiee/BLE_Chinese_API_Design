After a foreground write failure, that path must be removed immediately and the merged business peer must become not ready until a newer positive connection/subscription event arrives.

### Background Write Failure Is Not Equivalent To Disconnect

- 12:20:45.937 C sent `T9`.
- 12:20:46.027 `notify=success` with `subscribed=1`.
- 12:20:46.085 final result was success.
- 12:20:46.269 `write-bg peer=3d5c result=failed(写入失败: 1)`.
- Later sends such as `T10` and `B11` through `B15` succeeded, and C received `B13` through `B17`.

Interpretation: after notification succeeds, the background client write is only a redundant path. A `write-bg` failure should be logged but should not by itself mark the peer as disconnected.

## Conclusion For v0.6.5

- Keep `subscribed=0` allowed when foreground client write succeeds.
- Do not rely on raw BLE channel counts for business readiness.
- Persist a "business peer hard disconnect" timestamp after disconnect callbacks, independent of raw address record aging.
- When notification is unavailable and foreground client write fails, immediately remove that write path and mark the merged business peer not ready.
- Do not downgrade readiness for background write failures after notification success.
