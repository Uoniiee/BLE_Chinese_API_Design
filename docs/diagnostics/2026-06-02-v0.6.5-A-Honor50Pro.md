## Findings

1. The v0.6.5 business hard-disconnect guard works on A-side for the remote-disconnect case.
   After the 14:06:08 remote disconnect, a stale `6b18:c=true,w=true` record resurfaced at 14:07:48,
   but A did not return to business `ready=1`. Later send attempts were correctly skipped with
   `peer_not_ready`.

2. `subscribed=0` is still not a failure by itself.
   From 14:12:00 onward, A has `clients=1 subscribed=0`; notify fails, but direct write succeeds and
   app-level send results are successful.

3. A-side long-ish rapid sending after reconnect looks stable.
   `B8` to `B17` show repeated successful foreground direct writes, with app-level success.

4. There is one suspicious stop-flow item to compare with C-side logs.
   After A `stop-done` at 14:09:38, the captured log shows scan/discover/connect/subscribe and
   `ready=1` without a captured `start-click`. If this was not a manual restart missing from the
   screenshots, the API may still need a session/generation guard so stale scan or connect callbacks
   cannot complete after `停止通信()`.

## Current assessment before C-side comparison

A-side confirms the main v0.6.5 fix target for remote disconnect:
business readiness stays blocked after a remote disconnect, and stale physical channel records do not
restore `ready=1`.

The only unresolved A-side question is whether local stop can still be followed by a late in-flight
connection callback. This needs C-side correlation before deciding whether v0.6.6 is needed.
