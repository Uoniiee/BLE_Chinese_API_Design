## Preliminary Conclusion

A-side evidence shows v0.6.4 is not ready to merge into RPS.

What works:

- Initial connect gating works.
- Immediate remote-disconnect gating works.
- Local stop/start cleanup works.
- Reconnect can recover.

What fails:

- The merged business peer does not remember a remote disconnect strongly
  enough. After the disconnected raw address record ages out or disappears, an
  old client-side writable record can make `[READY]` return to
  `ready=1 ... 可发送`.
- A send attempted in that false-ready state can fail with:
  `没有已订阅通知的邻机` plus `写入失败：1`.

Likely fix direction after comparing C-side logs:

- Store a per-business-peer "last hard disconnect" timestamp independent of
  individual raw address records.
- Keep the peer non-ready until a newer successful handshake/subscribe/write
  updates the same business peer after that disconnect.
- On foreground client-write failure, remove or downgrade that specific writable
  path immediately. Do not let a failed writable path continue to count as
  `可发送`.
- Do not globally require `subscribed > 0`, because later A-side logs show
  client-write-only communication can still succeed.
